Compiled by Robert Carruth, a La Palma homeowner since 1992 and a member of the Citizen Committee on La Palma's Sustainability and Future. Carruth served as spokesperson for a group of residents who opposed Measure W in 2024, seeking to protect existing term limits from being increased by sitting council members who funded the Yes on W committee and stood to directly benefit from the change.

Several primary documents cited throughout this record — including consultant records, campaign finance filings, and sign-ordinance correspondence — were obtained via California Public Records Act requests filed by the author.

This site presents what the public record shows. It is not an official City of La Palma publication, and it is not affiliated with the City of La Palma or lapalmaca.gov.
