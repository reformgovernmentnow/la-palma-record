Why this matters

Each of the five threads on this site tells one part of La Palma's recent history. This page puts every dated event from all five side by side, in the order it actually happened, so patterns across threads — not just within them — are visible. Nothing here is new reporting; every entry links back to the fully sourced version on its home page. A few entries don't carry a specific calendar date on their home page (background context, named analysis sections); those are placed at the point in the sequence they best fit, and are labeled with their original name rather than an invented date.
