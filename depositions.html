Depositions: Byer v. City of La Palma

Deposition testimony from the discrimination and retaliation lawsuit that ended in an $8.4 million jury verdict against the City of La Palma.

ROSS BYER, PLAINTIFF, v. CITY OF LA PALMA, DEFENDANT  ·  ORANGE COUNTY SUPERIOR COURT, CASE NO. 30-2024-01394359-CU-OE-CJC  ·  VERDICT: DEC. 9, 2025

A jury found the City of La Palma liable for failing to prevent race and national-origin discrimination against Ross Byer, a former La Palma police officer, who alleged he was subjected to discrimination and retaliation by a supervisor. This page tracks deposition testimony from the case as transcripts become available to the La Palma Record, summarizing who each witness is, how they connect to the case, and what their testimony suggests about the conduct the jury found the City responsible for. View the signed jury verdict forms (PDF).

A note on completeness: This page reflects excerpts from seven depositions and one trial-testimony excerpt, not full transcripts. Six of the seven depositions were noticed and primarily questioned by plaintiff's counsel; readers should weigh that the excerpts available for review reflect what plaintiff's counsel chose to highlight, not a random sample of each transcript. Ronald Lopino's testimony is the last deposition transcript currently on file with the La Palma Record for this case. James Olson's testimony was given at trial, not in a pretrial deposition, and is presented separately for that reason. These transcripts were obtained through the Orange County Superior Court's online case access and record system. In some cases, the only records available through that system are excerpts; complete deposition transcripts are not always available to the public. Where a summary draws an inference beyond what the excerpt directly shows, that is flagged explicitly on that witness's page.
