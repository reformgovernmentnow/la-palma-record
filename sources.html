Public Records Requests (CPRA)

Many of the documents referenced across this site — personnel records, disbursement data, investigation summaries, consultant records, and campaign finance filings — were obtained through California Public Records Act requests under Government Code § 7920.000 et seq.

  • City of La Palma — General Counsel / City Clerk's office (records request contact information available on the City's official website)

A note on sourcing: The Measure W campaign finance facts are drawn directly from the FPPC Form 460, 497, and 470 filings on file with the La Palma City Clerk; the Tripepi Smith facts are drawn directly from the executed consultant agreement, purchase order, invoices, calendar records, and work product produced by the City under the Public Records Act; the Torres allegations are drawn directly from the filed complaint; the Casey allegations are drawn directly from the First Amended Complaint filed March 13, 2026 and the underlying government claims and rejection letters; and the 2019 term-limit account is drawn directly from the City Council's own certified minutes of August 20 and September 3, 2019. Allegations in the Torres and Casey complaints are allegations in pending cases that have not been answered on the merits, and are labeled as such throughout this site.

Filing your own request: Under California's Public Records Act, any resident can request most government records. Agencies generally must respond within 10 days indicating whether the records exist and will be produced. If you'd like a request template, reach out through the contact information on this site.
